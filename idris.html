# The Story of Prophet Idris (AS) ### The Truthful Prophet • The Patient Servant • The One Raised to a High Station ## Introduction: Who Was Idris (AS)? Prophet Idris (AS) was one of the prophets chosen by Allah (SWT). Although the Qur'an tells us only a little about his life, it gives us some remarkable descriptions of him. Allah (SWT) describes Idris (AS) as a **man of truth and a prophet**. Allah (SWT) also tells us that He raised him to a high station. Idris (AS) is also mentioned among the righteous servants who were patient. These descriptions reveal three important qualities of this prophet: - **Truthfulness:** He was known for his deep commitment to the truth. - **Prophethood:** Allah (SWT) chose him to be a prophet. - **Patience:** He was among those who remained steadfast. His story teaches us that a person's true worth is not measured by fame, wealth, or worldly power, but by faith and righteousness. --- # 1. Idris (AS) in the Qur'an Prophet Idris (AS) is mentioned by name in two places in the Qur'an. The first mention is in Surah Maryam. Allah (SWT) says: > **"And mention in the Book, Idris. Indeed, he was a man of truth and a prophet."** > > — Qur'an 19:56 This verse directly establishes that Idris (AS) was both truthful and a prophet. The second description follows immediately afterward: > **"And We raised him to a high station."** > > — Qur'an 19:57 These two verses are the foundation of what we know about Idris (AS). They do not give us a long biography, but they tell us that Allah (SWT) honored him and praised his character. We should be careful not to invent details about a prophet when Allah (SWT) has not revealed them to us. --- # 2. A Prophet Known for Truthfulness One of the most important qualities of Idris (AS) was his truthfulness. The Qur'an uses the word **siddiq** to describe him. This word conveys a person who is exceptionally truthful and sincere. Truthfulness is more than simply avoiding lies. A truthful person tries to make their words agree with reality, keeps promises, and does not deliberately mislead others. For a believer, truthfulness also means being sincere with Allah (SWT). A person should not pretend to be righteous in front of others while secretly choosing dishonesty. The description of Idris (AS) reminds us that truthfulness is a quality worth developing throughout our lives. We should speak honestly, admit our mistakes, and avoid making claims we cannot support. --- # 3. Allah (SWT) Chose Him as a Prophet Idris (AS) was not simply a good man. Allah (SWT) explicitly describes him as a prophet. Prophets were chosen by Allah (SWT) to receive revelation and guide people toward His worship. The Qur'an does not tell us the precise details of Idris's mission, the name of his people, or the exact words he preached to them. Therefore, we cannot confidently describe those details as established facts. What we can say with certainty is that Allah (SWT) honored Idris (AS) with prophethood. This reminds us that guidance is a tremendous blessing. Allah (SWT) sent prophets to teach people about their Creator, call them to righteousness, and warn them against wrongdoing. --- # 4. What Do We Know About His Life? Many popular stories about Idris (AS) describe events from his life in great detail. Some accounts connect him with the early generations of humanity. Others describe him as the inventor of writing, the teacher of particular crafts, or a person who introduced various forms of knowledge. However, these details are not established by clear, authentic evidence from the Qur'an and reliable prophetic narrations. We should distinguish between what Islamic revelation confirms and what later historical traditions report. The Qur'an does not provide a detailed account of Idris's childhood, family, birthplace, occupation, or everyday life. This does not make his story unimportant. Instead, it teaches us an important principle: **we should be honest about what we know and what we do not know.** When discussing the prophets, respecting the truth includes refusing to present uncertain stories as facts. --- # 5. Idris (AS) Was Among the Patient Allah (SWT) mentions Idris (AS) in Surah Al-Anbiya alongside two other righteous servants. Allah (SWT) says: > **"And [mention] Ishmael, Idris, and Dhul-Kifl; all were of the patient."** > > — Qur'an 21:85 This verse tells us that Idris (AS) was among those characterized by patience. Patience in Islam is not simply waiting for something to happen. It includes remaining steadfast in obedience to Allah (SWT), resisting wrongdoing, and continuing to do what is right even when it is difficult. We may experience moments when honesty costs us something, when doing the right thing is unpopular, or when our efforts seem to produce little progress. Patience helps us remain committed to what is right rather than giving up whenever life becomes difficult. Idris (AS) is a reminder of the importance of this quality. --- # 6. Allah (SWT) Included Him Among the Righteous The verse mentioning Idris (AS) continues with another important statement. Allah (SWT) says: > **"And We admitted them into Our mercy. Indeed, they were of the righteous."** > > — Qur'an 21:86 This verse refers to the prophets mentioned in the preceding passage, including Idris (AS). Allah (SWT) describes them as righteous and tells us that He admitted them into His mercy. Righteousness includes believing in Allah (SWT), obeying Him, and striving to live according to His guidance. It is not about merely appearing good to other people. A believer should seek Allah's pleasure through sincere faith and good actions. The verse also reminds us that Allah's mercy is a tremendous blessing. We should strive to do good while asking Allah (SWT) to forgive us and have mercy on us. --- # 7. What Does It Mean That Allah Raised Him to a High Station? Allah (SWT) says: > **"And We raised him to a high station."** > > — Qur'an 19:57 This is one of the most well-known statements about Idris (AS). Scholars have discussed the meaning of this verse and how it relates to his status. An authentic narration about the Night Journey of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ mentions that he met Idris (AS) in the fourth heaven. This meeting is recorded in authentic hadith collections, including Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim. Some scholars connect this narration with the Qur'anic statement about Idris being raised to a high station. However, the Qur'anic verse itself does not provide a complete explanation of every detail surrounding his elevation. We should therefore affirm what Allah (SWT) has told us while avoiding unsupported claims about precisely how every event occurred. What is clear is that Idris (AS) held an honored position. --- # 8. The Night Journey of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ Many years after the time of Idris (AS), Allah (SWT) granted Prophet Muhammad ﷺ the miraculous Night Journey and Ascension, known as the Isra and Mi'raj. During this extraordinary journey, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was taken through the heavens. According to authentic narrations, he met several prophets during his ascent. Among them was Idris (AS), whom he met in the fourth heaven. The narration describes the prophets welcoming Muhammad ﷺ and acknowledging his prophethood. This meeting is significant because it confirms that Idris (AS) is recognized among the prophets encountered during the Mi'raj. It also connects the brief Qur'anic account of Idris (AS) with an event described in the authentic Sunnah. The Qur'an mentions that Allah (SWT) raised Idris to a high station, while the authentic narrations give us this account of the meeting in the heavens. **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari, the narrations of the Night Journey and Ascension; Sahih Muslim, the Book of Faith. --- # 9. Did Idris (AS) Remain Alive in the Heavens? Some popular accounts claim that Idris (AS) was taken alive into the heavens and that the Angel of Death took his soul there. This story is sometimes presented as a definite explanation of Qur'an 19:57. However, the claim that Idris (AS) was raised alive in this particular manner is not established by an authentic narration from Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. Historical reports discussing this matter have been evaluated differently, and some scholars have noted weaknesses in them. We should therefore avoid stating as a certainty that Idris (AS) was taken alive into the heavens or that his death occurred in a specific place. The authentic narration of the Mi'raj establishes that Prophet Muhammad ﷺ met Idris (AS) in the fourth heaven. The exact circumstances behind Idris's elevation should not be described beyond what the reliable evidence supports. Allah (SWT) knows best. --- # 10. Was Idris (AS) the Same Person as Enoch? Idris (AS) is often identified with Enoch, a figure mentioned in the Bible. Many Muslim scholars have made this identification when discussing the history of the prophets. However, the Qur'an does not explicitly state that Idris (AS) and Enoch are the same person. It is therefore important to distinguish a widely held historical identification from something directly stated in the Qur'an. For our study of Idris (AS), we can focus on what the Islamic sources clearly establish: he was a prophet, a man of truth, one of the patient, and a servant whom Allah (SWT) raised to a high station. We do not need to settle every historical question to benefit from the lessons his story provides. --- # 11. When and Where Did Idris (AS) Live? The Qur'an does not give us an exact date or geographical location for the life of Idris (AS). Many traditional accounts place him among the earliest generations of humanity, before Prophet Nuh (AS). However, the exact chronology of his life is not established by a clear, authentic narration that settles the matter. Similarly, the Qur'an does not identify the precise city or country where he lived. We should be cautious about claiming that he was born in a particular place or lived during a specific year without reliable evidence. This is an important lesson for anyone studying Islamic history. Not every question has a definite answer available to us. Sometimes the most accurate answer is simply that Allah (SWT) has not informed us of the detail. --- # 12. Did Idris (AS) Invent Writing? A common story about Idris (AS) credits him with inventing writing. Other traditional accounts associate him with sewing, astronomy, or different crafts. These stories have appeared in later historical and religious literature. However, the Qur'an and authentic hadith do not establish these inventions as facts about Idris (AS). We should therefore avoid presenting them as confirmed parts of his biography. This does not mean that Idris (AS) lacked knowledge or that he did not perform important deeds. It simply means that we should not attribute specific achievements to a prophet without reliable evidence. Truthfulness requires us to be careful even when a story sounds inspiring. --- # 13. Why Is His Story So Brief? At first, the short account of Idris (AS) might seem surprising. Other prophets have much longer stories in the Qur'an, while the information about Idris (AS) is limited to a few verses and the authentic narrations that mention him. But the Qur'an is not intended to provide a complete chronological biography of every prophet. It provides guidance, reminders, and lessons for humanity. The account of Idris (AS) emphasizes his truthfulness, prophethood, patience, righteousness, and elevated station. These qualities are enough to teach us important lessons even without a detailed account of his daily life. A story does not have to contain hundreds of events to be meaningful. Sometimes a few words from Allah (SWT) carry lessons that can guide a person for an entire lifetime. --- # 14. The Importance of Truthfulness The first major quality highlighted in the Qur'anic account of Idris (AS) is truthfulness. This quality matters in every part of our lives. At school, truthfulness means admitting when we have not completed an assignment instead of making excuses. At home, it means being honest with our family. Among friends, it means keeping promises and refusing to spread lies. When learning about Islam, it means distinguishing authentic teachings from rumors and unsupported stories. Truthfulness can sometimes be difficult, especially when we fear the consequences of admitting a mistake. But honesty builds trust, strengthens character, and helps us become better people. Idris (AS) gives us an example of a prophet whose truthfulness was specifically praised by Allah (SWT). --- # 15. The Importance of Patience The Qur'an also identifies Idris (AS) as one of the patient. Patience is needed in worship, learning, relationships, and the challenges of everyday life. A person learning something difficult may need to practise many times before improving. Someone trying to change a bad habit may experience setbacks. A believer may face hardship while trying to do what is right. Patience does not mean that a person never feels frustrated or sad. It means continuing to seek what is right rather than allowing frustration to lead to wrongdoing. Idris (AS) reminds us that patience is a quality praised by Allah (SWT). --- # 16. Respecting What We Do Not Know One of the most valuable lessons from studying Idris (AS) is the importance of intellectual honesty. It can be tempting to fill gaps in a story with interesting details. A person might hear a report repeated many times and assume that it must be authentic. But repetition alone does not prove that something is true. When studying the prophets, we should prioritize the Qur'an and authentic Sunnah. If a historical report is uncertain, we should explain that uncertainty rather than presenting the report as established fact. This approach does not diminish the prophets. Instead, it shows respect for them and for the revelation through which Allah (SWT) taught us about them. **Knowing when not to make a claim is part of being truthful.** --- # 17. Lessons We Can Learn from Idris (AS) There are several important lessons we can take from the Qur'anic account of Idris (AS). **1. Be truthful.** Honesty should guide our words, actions, promises, and relationships. **2. Be patient.** Continue doing what is right even when it takes time or becomes difficult. **3. Seek righteousness.** Try to obey Allah (SWT) sincerely rather than focusing only on how others see you. **4. Remember that honor comes from Allah (SWT).** A person's true worth is not determined by wealth, popularity, or social position. **5. Learn from authentic sources.** When studying Islam, distinguish reliable evidence from stories whose authenticity is uncertain. **6. Admit the limits of your knowledge.** It is better to say that something is unknown than to confidently spread an unsupported claim. **7. Ask Allah (SWT) for mercy.** Even when we strive to do good, we remain in need of Allah's guidance and mercy. --- # 18. What the Qur'an Teaches Us About Idris (AS) The Qur'anic account of Idris (AS) is short, but its message is clear. Allah (SWT) identifies him as a truthful man and a prophet. Allah (SWT) tells us that He raised him to a high station. Elsewhere, Idris (AS) is named among the patient, and Allah (SWT) describes the prophets in that passage as righteous servants admitted into His mercy. The authentic Sunnah also records that Prophet Muhammad ﷺ met Idris (AS) during the Night Journey and Ascension. Beyond these established details, many aspects of his earthly life remain unknown to us. We should not allow uncertainty to distract us from the qualities that Allah (SWT) chose to highlight. Truthfulness, patience, faith, and righteousness remain valuable lessons for every generation. --- # 19. Conclusion: A Prophet Remembered for Truth Prophet Idris (AS) is one of the prophets whose story reminds us that even a brief mention in revelation can carry a powerful message. Allah (SWT) praised his truthfulness. Allah (SWT) identified him as a prophet. Allah (SWT) described him as one of the patient. And Allah (SWT) told us that He raised him to a high station. The authentic narrations of the Night Journey further tell us that Prophet Muhammad ﷺ met Idris (AS) in the fourth heaven. Although we do not know every detail of Idris's earthly life, we know enough to understand the importance of the qualities associated with him. We should strive to speak the truth, remain patient, do good, and seek the mercy of Allah (SWT). We should also be careful when sharing religious stories, making sure that what we attribute to Allah (SWT) and His prophets is supported by reliable evidence. The story of Idris (AS) is a reminder that truthfulness and patience are not small qualities. They are qualities that Allah (SWT) praised in His chosen prophet. And they are qualities that every one of us can strive to develop. --- # Sources and References **The Qur'an** - Surah Maryam, 19:56–57 — Idris (AS) as a truthful prophet and his elevated station. - Surah Al-Anbiya, 21:85–86 — Idris (AS) among the patient and righteous. **Authentic Hadith** - Sahih al-Bukhari — Narrations of the Isra and Mi'raj, including the meeting with Idris (AS). - Sahih Muslim — Narrations of the Night Journey and Ascension. **Additional study** - [Read Surah Maryam, verses 56–57](https://quran.com/19/56-57) - [Read Surah Al-Anbiya, verses 85–86](https://quran.com/21/85-86) - [Explore narrations about Idris on Sunnah.com](https://sunnah.com/search?q=Idris) --- ## A Final Reminder The Qur'an does not tell us every detail about every prophet. What it does tell us is guidance. In the story of Idris (AS), we find a reminder to be truthful, patient, righteous, and careful with the knowledge we share.
